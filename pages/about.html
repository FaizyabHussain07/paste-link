About PasteLink Pro

Why PasteLink Exists

In a world where data is constantly being scraped, tracked, and monetized, we needed a place to share text quickly without leaving a permanent digital footprint. PasteLink was born out of the necessity for a truly frictionless, registration-free, and privacy-first text sharing tool.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide the fastest and most secure way to share snippets of text, code, or sensitive information on the internet, ensuring that the sender always retains control over their data's lifecycle.

Privacy First Philosophy

We do not store passwords in plaintext. We do not track your IP address for advertising. We do not sell your data. Every paste can be set to auto-expire or burn-after-read, ensuring that once it's gone, it's gone forever.
